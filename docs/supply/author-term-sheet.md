# Flipbook — Author Terms

**One page. Plain English. If a lawyer needs to translate this, we've failed.**

**Version:** v1 (July 2026 — founding-author cohort)
**Parties:** Flipbook (the app) and **[Author Name]** (you)

---

## 1. What Flipbook does with your book

- We host your book in the Flipbook app as a **rental**. Readers rent it for a fixed period. They never own a copy.
- Readers cannot download, export, print, or share the file. All reading happens inside the app.
- We do not sell your book. We do not resell your book. We rent it.

## 2. What you get paid

- **70% of every rental fee** goes to you. Flipbook keeps 30%.
- Your book is assigned to one of three rental bands at signing:
  - **Band A — ₦500 per 4-week rental** — public-domain and access-tier titles (usually not indie).
  - **Band B — ₦1,500 per 4-week rental** — standard indie titles.
  - **Band C — ₦2,500 per 4-week rental** — premium indie and small-publisher titles.
- Example: at Band B, you earn **₦1,050 per rental**. At Band C, **₦1,750 per rental**.
- Pro subscribers get 15% off Band B, C, D. Your 70% share is calculated on the **actual price paid** by the reader, not the list price.

## 3. When you get paid

- We pay you **once a month**, on or before the 15th, covering the previous calendar month's rentals.
- Payment via **bank transfer (NGN)** or **PayPal / Wise (USD)** — your choice at signing.
- No minimum payout threshold. If you earned ₦300 last month, you get ₦300 this month.
- Every payment comes with a statement: rentals count, average rental length, gross revenue, your share.

## 4. Rental mechanics (for your reader)

- Standard rental period: **4 weeks**.
- Pro subscribers get **6 weeks**.
- No extensions on a single rental. If the reader wants more time, they re-rent — and you get paid again.

## 4b. Audio narration (Pro feature)

- Flipbook generates a neural-TTS audio version of every catalog book, available only to Pro subscribers. This lets readers listen instead of read.
- **You can opt out.** Tick the box at signing and your book will be text-only on Flipbook. No AI narration will ever be produced for it, and no reader will hear a machine voice reading your prose.
- If you opt in (default), you receive **the same 70% rental share on every Pro rental**, whether the reader read the text, listened to the audio, or both. Audio listening does not generate a separate rental — it's part of the same 4- or 6-week rental window.
- If Flipbook ever offers **human-narrated audio** of your book later, that's a separate opt-in conversation with separate terms. This clause covers AI/TTS only.

## 5. Exclusivity

- **Non-exclusive by default.** You keep publishing anywhere you already do — Amazon KDP, Okada Books, Selar, your own site, print, everywhere. Flipbook does not ask for a cut of anything you sell elsewhere.
- **Flipbook Specials (optional).** If you choose to put a title in Flipbook Specials — our curated free-to-Pro tier — that title is exclusive to Flipbook for a **90-day window**, and we pay you a flat **₦15,000 per title per quarter** during that window instead of the per-rental share. You can opt any of your other titles into Specials later. Specials placement is by mutual agreement, never by default.

## 6. Your rights

- **You own your book. Always.** You retain all copyright, all subsidiary rights, all everything. We license non-exclusive digital distribution for the rental only.
- You can pull your book from Flipbook with **30 days' written notice**, no reason required. Existing active rentals run out; no new rentals from the pull date.
- You can update your book's file (typos, revisions, new cover) any time. We publish the update within 5 business days.

## 7. Piracy and file protection

- Every rental copy is watermarked with the reader's account ID (invisibly).
- If a Flipbook rental file appears elsewhere on the internet, we investigate, we tell you, and we take action on the reader account. This is a promise, not a legal covenant.
- We do not use DRM that would break the reader's experience. We use in-app-only rendering, watermarking, and account-level enforcement.

## 8. Content

- You certify your book is your work, that you have the rights to license it, and that it doesn't contain content that violates Nigerian law or the app store policies (no CSAM, no incitement, no straight plagiarism).
- We reserve the right to remove a book that violates the above, with notice to you and reason given.

## 9. The founding-author promise

- You are among the **first 30 authors** on Flipbook.
- Your book gets **featured placement** in the September launch shelf.
- Your name and cover appear in **founding-author launch marketing** — we ask first, you approve every use.
- Your rental band gets a **review at every subscriber milestone** — 500 Pro, 2,000 Pro, 10,000 Pro. Anywhere the economics justify a band raise, we raise it.

## 10. The term

- This agreement runs **12 months** from signing, then auto-renews for 12-month periods unless either side gives 30 days' notice.
- Either side can end it any time with 30 days' notice, no penalty. Rentals in progress at end date are honored.
- If Flipbook shuts down, all rights revert to you immediately. We're not going to hold your book hostage.

## Notes to Flipbook staff (delete before sending to author)

- Bands are set at signing by editorial + author. Default assignment: novellas + collections start Band B; full novels with prior sales history start Band C; unpublished or debut-only start Band B.
- Specials opt-in is a separate email conversation after the base agreement is signed. Don't bundle.
- For the first 30 authors: consider offering a **founding-author bonus** — one extra 10% of rentals (so 80/20) for the first six months. Not in the base sheet; case-by-case in negotiation.
- Payment infrastructure: at signing, capture bank details for NGN payouts (Paystack Transfers) or PayPal/Wise ID for USD. Store encrypted; never in shared docs.
