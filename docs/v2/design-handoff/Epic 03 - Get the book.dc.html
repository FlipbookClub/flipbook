Get the book

Every screen, flow and state for stories 3.1 to 3.7: the confirmation sheet and both payment routes, Pro pricing, failed payments, the Shelf, rental expiry, takedowns and gifting. Flows read left to right in Flip; key screens repeat in Light and Dark, then in Classic and on Android.

3.1 · P0 · Browser

Getting a book takes seconds and never feels like a checkout

One sheet confirms the price and the rental end date, then the book opens on the next chapter. No receipt screen. The payment route is still open (§8), so the payment step is a wireframe in both variants; everything around it is final.
Timing for a returning reader
Tap Get it, confirm, pay, read: four steps, about 20 seconds with a saved method. The target is under 45.
The button holds its pressed fill with “Opening” while payment clears; the reader replaces the sheet directly.
A toast in the reader confirms the rental end date. That is the only receipt.
