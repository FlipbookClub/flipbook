Tablet

Every main screen from Epics 1 to 12 on iPad (landscape, portrait, Split View and Slide Over) and Android tablets, with key states: offline, empty and sheets. Same components and copy as the phone; the layout adapts to the space available.

System

How the layout adapts

One set of screens across every width.
Layout rules for every large screen
Layout follows the space the app has, never the device: under 600 wide is the phone layout with the floating tab bar; 600 to 999 adds a left icon rail; 1000 and up gets the full sidebar with your communities and Continue reading.
Content reflows in cards that wrap (minimum 300 to 330 wide), so the same screen works at every width and mid-resize.
The Reader always goes full-screen with no navigation. From 700 wide it becomes a two-page spread; margin reactions sit in the gutter beside their paragraph.
Sheets become centred form sheets, 440 wide, on wide windows; on compact windows they stay bottom sheets.
Primary action at most once per view; Flip theme shown, Light, Dark and Classic follow the same tokens.
