iPhone Duo

Built for the iOS 27.1 SDK behaviour in Apple’s iPhone Duo guide: the app extends edge to edge on both displays, the status bar sits on the right edge, and the tab bar runs vertically below it. Closed, Flipbook uses its phone layout; open, it uses the same layout as iPad. Display sizes are proportional placeholders until Apple publishes specs.

From Apple’s guide

What this adaptation follows

Points from “Three steps to make your app shine on iPhone Duo”, applied to Flipbook.
Rules
Size from the window, not the screen, and re-layout whenever it changes: a fold can resize the app mid-session.
No layout decisions from orientation, idiom or a specific device size. Flipbook picks phone, rail or sidebar layouts from available width only.
Open, the app should look like a natural extension of its iPad layout. Flipbook’s open layout uses the same cards, spread and wrapping rules as iPad.
Toolbars and tab bars run vertically below the status bar on the right edge. Flipbook’s four destinations become a vertical glass bar there; content must adapt to the bar being vertical.
Check full-bleed media on wider displays: covers always fit, never crop.
Continuity

Closed to open, same screen

Opening the phone resizes the current screen in place; the reader stays on the same paragraph and the vertical bar stays put on the right.
