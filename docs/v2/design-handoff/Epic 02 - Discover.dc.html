Home shows a few curated shelves

Continue reading, From your communities, New this week, Specials, Classics and In your genres. Each shelf scrolls sideways and ends; there is no feed.
Who shows up in “From your communities” Flipbook has no contacts or friends list, so the people on this shelf and in “Ada and 2 others are reading this” are always members of communities you share.
Only people from your communities, never strangers.
Only books they’ve opened. Owning a copy alone never shows, so nobody learns who hasn’t bought a book.
Readers can turn this off in You › Privacy: “Show what I’m reading to my communities”, on by default.
Teens appear to, and see, only the communities they were invited into.
No shared communities means no shelf and no “reading this” line. Home shows the invite card instead.
