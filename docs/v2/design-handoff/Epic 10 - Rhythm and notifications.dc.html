Rhythm and notifications

Every screen and state for stories 10.1 to 10.3: notifications about your book, one Activity page for what happened while you were away, and a private reading rhythm. Flows read left to right in Flip; key screens repeat in Light and Dark, then in Classic and on Android.

10.1 · P0

Reminders are about my book

Every notification names a book, a chapter or a person, and opens straight to it. Every type can be switched off in You › Notifications.
Send
“Ada left a note in Chapter 5. That’s where you’re headed next.”
“Session in 1 hour. It covers up to Chapter 8; you’re in Chapter 9.”
“Kemi replied to your reaction in Chapter 3.”
Spoiler rules apply: a note ahead of you names only the chapter, never the text.
Never send
Guilt: “You haven’t read in 3 days.”
Fake urgency: “Only a few hours left!” when nothing ends.
Empty pushes: “Come back to Flipbook.” If there’s nothing to open, there’s no notification.
Counts, streaks or comparisons with other readers.

One place for what happened while I was away
