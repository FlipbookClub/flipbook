9.1 · P0

Pro shows up where it helps, not through nagging

Five moments, each tied to something the reader just tried to do, plus one entry in You. Nothing on launch, no interstitials, no countdowns. Every moment has a clear way out.
Rules for every Pro moment
Lead with what the reader was trying to do, then what Pro adds. Never a list of everything.
One primary action. “Not now” or the free path is always on the same screen.
Shown once per moment per session. Dismissing it never removes the free path.
Never on app launch, never mid-chapter, never on someone else’s screen.
